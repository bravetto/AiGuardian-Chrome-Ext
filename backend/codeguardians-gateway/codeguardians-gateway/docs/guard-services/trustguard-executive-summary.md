# TrustGuard - Executive Summary

## 🎯 **Business Overview**

**TrustGuard** is an AI reliability service that detects 7 critical AI failure patterns with 95%+ accuracy. It provides mathematical validation and constitutional prompting to ensure AI outputs are trustworthy, reliable, and safe for enterprise use.

## 🔍 **Core Capabilities**

### **AI Failure Pattern Detection**
- **Hallucination Detection**: Identifies false or fabricated information
- **Bias Amplification**: Detects and prevents bias in AI outputs
- **Context Drift**: Monitors conversation consistency
- **Confidence Miscalibration**: Identifies overconfident or underconfident responses
- **Adversarial Vulnerability**: Detects potential security threats
- **Temporal Inconsistency**: Ensures time-based accuracy
- **Domain Boundary**: Prevents out-of-scope responses

### **Mathematical Validation**
- **KL Divergence**: Statistical analysis of AI output distributions
- **Uncertainty Quantification**: Measures AI confidence levels
- **Risk Assessment**: Calculates potential impact of AI decisions
- **Statistical Analysis**: Comprehensive mathematical validation
